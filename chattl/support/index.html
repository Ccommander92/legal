Is my data private?

Yes. Everything you enter — items, photos, receipts, values, notes — stays on your device in Chattl's private storage. There is no cloud and no server. Chattl has no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. We cannot see your inventory.

Do I need an account?

No. Chattl has no accounts and no login. You open the app and start using it. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to remember.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. Chattl never needs an internet connection to work. The only time it goes online is when you make the optional one-time purchase, which is handled by the App Store or Google Play. Even receipt text recognition and barcode scanning run entirely on your device.

How do I back up my data? (.chattl export)

Use Export → .chattl archive. This creates a single .chattl file that is a full ZIP backup of your inventory, including photos and receipts. You can open it without the app, store it wherever you like, and use it to restore your data later. CSV and PDF exports are also available. Exports are free and never locked behind a purchase.

What is the PDF report?

The PDF report is Chattl's main output: a clean, adjuster-ready document of your belongings, room by room, with photos, descriptions, values, and receipts. If you ever need to make an insurance claim after a burglary, fire, or flood, you can generate this report and hand it straight to your insurer or adjuster.

What does Chattl Unlimited unlock?

Every feature in Chattl is free, including all exports. The free version lets you catalogue up to 30 items. Chattl Unlimited is a single one-time purchase (no subscription) that removes that limit so you can add as many items as you like. It supports Family Sharing / Play family library.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Export a .chattl archive on your old phone, transfer that file to your new phone (for example via AirDrop, a cable, a file transfer, or your own storage), install Chattl, and import the archive. Your data moves with you. Portability is never behind the paywall.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Item photos and receipt images stay on your device and are never uploaded. When you import a photo, Chattl also removes its location data (EXIF/GPS) so that information does not travel with the image inside your inventory.
