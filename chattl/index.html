Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

The short version: Chattl does not collect your data. Everything you put into the app stays on your device. There is no account, no cloud, and no tracking. We cannot see your inventory, your photos, your receipts, or anything else you enter.

What data Chattl stores, and where

You can create the following in Chattl, all stored only on your device in the app's private storage:

  • Properties / home details you choose to add (optional address, policy number, insurer)
  • Rooms
  • Items (name, brand, model, serial number, barcode, purchase price, replacement value, condition, notes, warranty date)
  • Photos of items and receipt images

None of this is transmitted to us or to any third party. We have no server that receives it.

What Chattl does NOT do

  • No user accounts and no login.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no advertising identifiers.
  • No crash-reporting or telemetry SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no data leaving your device to sell or share.

Networking

The only network activity is the in-app purchase ("Chattl Unlimited"), which is processed by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). Payment is handled entirely by the store; Chattl does not receive or store your payment details. Apple's and Google's handling of purchases is governed by their own privacy policies.

On-device processing

  • Receipt text recognition (OCR) runs on your device using on-device machine learning. Receipt images are not uploaded.
  • Barcode scanning runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Photo import removes location metadata (EXIF/GPS) from imported photos.

Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph items and scan barcodes and receipts.
  • Photo library — to import existing photos.
  • Face ID / fingerprint — optional app lock only.

Chattl does not request location, microphone, or contacts access.

Your control over your data

  • Export: at any time you can export your data as a .chattl archive (a full backup you can open without the app), a CSV file, or a PDF report. Export is never restricted.
  • Delete: you can delete any item, room, property, photo, or receipt inside the app.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes all Chattl data from the device.

Because we never receive your data, there is no server-side copy for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf.

Children

Chattl is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised policy at the same URL.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@chattl.app
